Installing takes a minute

The apps are in testing and are shared as an APK file, not through Google Play.

  1. Tap Download APK above for the app you need.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  3. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open the app and log in with your mobile number.

Questions

Is the app safe to install?

Each APK is signed by us. Compare the checksum shown under the download button with the file on your phone if you want to be sure.

Why isn't it on Google Play?

We are testing with a small group first. A Play Store release comes after testing.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Apna Ride is Android only for now.

I don't get my OTP.

Wait a minute, then tap Resend OTP. Check that your number is correct.
